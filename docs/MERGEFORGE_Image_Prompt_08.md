## Prompt

Create a polished 16:9 concept-art gameplay overview for the browser strategy game MERGEFORGE. Use the reference only for simplified modern digital browser-game “Diorama Lite” style: readable low-poly/isometric hex terrain, clean shapes, moderate detail, warm miniature buildings, blue-green landscape.

Two other player villages are tiny distant silhouettes at the far upper-left and far upper-right, at least 15–20 hexes away and largely hidden by fog. Do not connect players by road or bridge.

No armies, no combat, no huge castles, no dense city, no sci-fi factory, no floating islands, no separate scenes, no title or paragraphs of text.
